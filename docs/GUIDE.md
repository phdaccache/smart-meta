# How to use Smart Meta

## The five pieces

| Piece | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | A change you're making, with a *why* | Work at a big tech, earning 10k+ |
| **Commitment** | A checkable action inside a goal. The only thing that's scored. | Solved 2 LeetCode problems, 4× a week |
| **Prep** | A small step that makes a commitment easier. Never scored. | Sunday 20:00: pick this week's 3 companies |
| **Project** | Finite work with steps. Today shows only the next step. | CV & LinkedIn |
| **Task** | A single action | Ask Ana for a referral |

**Which one is it?** If you'll keep doing it, it's a commitment. If it's finished once it's done, it's a project; if it's a single action, it's a task.

## Worked example: "Work at a big tech, earning 10k+"

You can't check an outcome every day, but you can check the behaviors that get you there.

**1. Create the goal** (Goals → **+**). The wizard walks through SMART:

| | You enter |
|---|---|
| **S**pecific | "Work at a big tech, earning 10k+" · Rhythm · "Sent at least one tailored application" |
| **M**easurable | 3× per week |
| **A**chievable | Tolerance 70%: you need to hit 70% of your check-ins to be on track |
| **R**elevant | Value: Career · "Work on things millions use, and never worry about money again." |
| **T**ime-bound | Review in 12 months |
| Prep | "Pick this week's 3 companies", Sunday 20:00 |

**2. Add the other behaviors.** On the goal's page, go to Commitments → **Add**:
- LeetCode: "Solved at least 2 LeetCode problems", 4× a week.
- Mock interview: "Did a mock interview with a friend", 1× a week, with the prep "Book a friend", Monday 21:00.

**3. Add projects for finite work** (Goals → New project → Goal: this one):
- CV & LinkedIn: update CV → get it reviewed → update LinkedIn → list 20 companies.
- System design prep: primer → URL shortener → chat app → news feed → 2 timed designs.
- Interview stories: list 8 situations → write STAR stories → rehearse → record one.

**4. Add tasks for one-offs.** Tap **+**, type "Ask Ana for a referral", use 📅 to pick tomorrow and 🎯 to pick this goal.

**5. Leave "general tech study" for later.** If you're at your active-goal limit, it waits in the **backlog** as its own goal. Start it when a slot opens.

## Every day (under a minute)

Open **Today** and tick what you did. Everything from this goal sits under its *why*. A finished goal collapses to one line.

## When you miss

On Monday you see "Last week: 2 of 4 LeetCode. What happened?". Tap **Chose something else**, then add what took its place, e.g. "gaming". Next time "gaming" is one tap. These reasons will feed the weekly Review screen once it exists.

**Forgot to log?** Tap 📅 at the top of Today, pick the day, and tick things there.

## Every week

Open **Goals**. Each goal shows **on track / behind / at risk** and a bar for the last 4 weeks. If a goal slips, you can:
- lower a commitment's target (4× → 3×),
- add a prep,
- or move a goal to the backlog.

Edits are kept in the goal's history.

## Review day

When the review date arrives, the goal asks: *Did you hit it? What happened?* It also asks for a short journal note. Then you choose **Renew** (set a new date), **Maintenance** (keep going without an end date) or **Close it out**.

## Try it without waiting

In **Settings → Developer** (temporary):
- **Load sample data** replaces everything with this example plus a few other goals and three weeks of history.
- **Pretend today is** moves the app to any date. Set next Monday to see last week's prompts, or 12 months ahead to reach review day. Tap the orange pill to go back to the real date.
