# How to use Smart Meta

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Something you want, with a *why*. It has one of two shapes, below. | Work at a big tech, earning 10k+ |
| **Habit** (commitment) | A checkable action you repeat. The only thing that's scored. | 2 LeetCode problems, 4× a week |
| **Prep** | A small step that makes a habit easier. Never scored. | Monday 21:00: book a friend for the mock |
| **Project** | Finite work with steps. Today shows only the next step. | CV & LinkedIn |
| **Task** | A single action | Ask Ana for a referral |

**Two kinds of goal**
- **Habit goal:** you keep doing something ("Exercise regularly"). It's measured by its habit's check-ins, and its slack is a tolerance (e.g. 80% of check-ins).
- **Finish-line goal:** you reach something once ("Get the job"). It's measured by one yes/no question, and its slack is extra time after the deadline. Habits are optional helpers.

## Worked example: "Work at a big tech, earning 10k+"

**1. Create it** (Plan → Goals → **+**). The wizard lights up S M A R T as you go:

| | You enter |
|---|---|
| **S** Specific | Work at a big tech, earning 10k+ |
| **M** Measurable | *Finish line* · done when "Signed an offer from a big tech company paying at least 10k a month" |
| **A** Achievable | Extra time: 2 months |
| **R** Relevant | Career · "Work on things millions use, and never worry about money again." |
| **T** Time-bound | Deadline in 12 months |

New goals wait in the **backlog**. Open the goal and tap **Start**.

**2. Add supporting habits** on the goal page (Supporting habits → **Add**). Each one has its own start date:
- LeetCode: "Solved at least 2 LeetCode problems", 4× a week, starting now.
- Mock interview: "Did a mock interview with a friend", once a week, with the prep "Book a friend", Monday 21:00.
- Applications: "Sent at least one tailored application", 3× a week, **starting in 2 months**. It stays off Today until then, so you can prepare first.

**3. Add projects** on the goal page (Projects → **Add**):
- CV & LinkedIn: update CV → get it reviewed → update LinkedIn → list 20 companies.
- System design prep: primer → URL shortener → chat app → news feed → 2 timed designs.
- Interview stories: list 8 situations → write STAR stories → rehearse → record one.

Drag the ⠿ grip to reorder steps.

**4. One-offs:** tap **+**, type "Ask Ana for a referral", then use 📅 for a due date and 🎯 for the goal. To add a step to a project instead, pick the project with ☰.

**5. General tech study** is its own habit goal ("Keep up with tech"). It waits in the backlog until a slot opens.

## Every day (under a minute)

On **Today**, tick what you did. Each goal's items sit under its *why*. Use the **All / Goals / Projects / Tasks** switch to focus on one kind. A finished goal collapses to one line.

Rule-type habits (like "be on time") have nothing to tick. Their goal shows a **Log** row; tap it after an appointment.

## When you miss

After a week closes you see "Last week: 2 of 4 LeetCode. What happened?". Tap **Chose something else**, then add what took its place, e.g. "gaming". Next time it's one tap.

**Forgot to log?** Tap 📅 at the top of Today, pick the day, and tick things there. A goal's full history is behind the 🕘 icon at the top right of its page (✓ done, ✕ missed, ↺ corrected, ✎ edited).

## Every week

Open **Plan**. Each goal card shows its status (**on track / behind / at risk**), its habits, and its projects. Drag cards to change their order on Today. If a goal slips, you can:
- lower a habit's target,
- add a prep,
- or move the goal to the backlog.

For finish-line goals, the status comes from their habits until the deadline. It turns **behind** during the extra time and **at risk** after it.

## The end

- **Finish line:** tap **I did it** on the goal page whenever you get it. If the deadline and extra time run out first, the goal asks you to review it.
- **Habit goal with a review date:** on that date the goal asks: *Did you hit it? What happened?* It also asks for a journal note.

Then choose **Renew**, **Maintenance** (habit goals only) or **Close it out**.

## Try it without waiting

In **Settings → Developer** (temporary):
- **Load sample data** replaces everything with this example plus other goals and three weeks of history.
- **Pretend today is** moves the app to any date. Tap the orange pill to go back to the real date.
