Where the numbers come from

Limits checked on 2026-10-21. Supabase Free: 500 MB database (read-only above it), 5 GB egress, 1 GB files, 50,000 monthly users, 500,000 Edge Function calls, no backups. Pro: $25 a month with 8 GB, then $0.125 per GB, 250 GB egress then $0.09 per GB, daily backups. supabase.com/pricing

Vercel Hobby: 100 GB transfer, 1 million function calls, 50,000 Web Analytics events a month (collection pauses past it; Hobby is never billed). vercel.com/docs/limits

GitHub Free private repos: 2,000 Actions minutes and 500 MB of artifact storage a month, used here for the encrypted backups.

Record sizes were measured on the app's sample data (JSON per record): check-ins 290 B, goals 410 B, commitments 400 B, tasks 250 B, projects 245 B, preps 270 B, occurrences 310 B, reviews 195 B, revisions 290 B. Each row also costs about 250 B in Postgres for its columns, header and two indexes. Dumps are assumed to compress to 20%.

A dispatcher that runs every minute costs about 43,200 Edge Function calls a month no matter how many users there are. Push delivery through Apple and Google is free.

Estimates, not a bill. Calibrate with your real database size once friends are on it.
